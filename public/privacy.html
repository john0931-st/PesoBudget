PesoBudget Smart Philippine Expense & Payroll Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · App URL: https://pesobudget-ph.web.app/

1. Information We Access

PesoBudget is a personal payroll and budget spreadsheet application. When you use the app offline or without signing in, your spreadsheet rows, categories, notes, and settings stay strictly inside your browser's local storage on your device.

If you choose to sign in with Google for Cloud Backup & Restore, we receive:

  • Your Google account profile (name, verified email address, user ID, and profile photo).
  • Your saved budget sheets and cloud backup snapshots that you choose to sync to Firebase Firestore.
  • Optional Gmail send permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send), used solely to deliver your 6-digit verification code to your own Gmail inbox during sign-in or Portable Password Lock setup.

2. How Gmail Permissions & User Data Are Used

  • PesoBudget does not read, scan, index, or delete your Gmail messages. The gmail.send permission is used strictly to send a 6-digit one-time verification code to your own signed-in email address when requested by you.
  • OAuth access tokens are kept strictly in active browser memory for your current session and are never stored in localStorage, sessionStorage, or shared with third parties.
  • Cloud data in Firebase Firestore is isolated per user ID so only your authenticated account can read, update, or delete your sheets and backups.
  • PesoBudget does not sell, rent, or use your personal or financial data for advertising or third-party AI training.

3. Local Encryption, Biometrics & Data Deletion

Optional Portable Password Lock encryption (AES-GCM / PBKDF2) and optional device Fingerprint Biometrics (WebAuthn) run locally on your device. You may sign out, clear browser storage, or delete your cloud backup snapshots inside Settings at any time, or request account deletion by contacting the developer below.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the app developer at archiejp21@gmail.com.
